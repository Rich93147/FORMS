# Datasets

Datasets are not redistributed; download them into `data/raw/<name>/` (git-ignored). I will add official download links at a later time.

| Dataset | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **KIMORE** | Primary training/evaluation | Kinect v2 RGB-D and skeleton data, 5 exercises including squat, 78 participants, clinician quality scores. |
| **UI-PRMD** | Secondary; validation of joint angles | 10 participants x 10 movements x 10 reps, Kinect + Vicon. Includes deep squat, lunge, sit-to-stand, straight-leg raise, shoulder movements. |
| **MSRC-12** | Early pipeline testing | 594 sequences, 719,359 frames, 30 people, 12 gestures, joints at 30 Hz. Not a form dataset. |

Check each dataset's license and citation requirements before use.
